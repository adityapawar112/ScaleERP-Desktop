# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Here are answers to the most common questions about using ScaleERP.

---

### Q1: Does ScaleERP require internet connection to run?
**Answer:** No. ScaleERP is designed to operate completely offline. All transaction ledgers, inventory counts, and license checks are stored locally on your computer. Internet is only needed if you want to connect your Google Drive for cloud backups or send messages using WhatsApp Web fallback.

---

### Q2: How can I print two copies of an invoice on a single A4 page?
**Answer:**
1. Go to **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Under *Invoice Template & Styling*, change **Print Copies** to **2 Copies**.
3. Under **Multi-Copy Layout Mode**, select **Single A4 Sheet (Dual Stacked with Scissor Line)**.
4. Click **Save All Business Settings**. When you click print, ScaleERP will stack two identical invoices on one A4 page with a dotted cut marker.

---

### Q3: Where are my backup files stored?
**Answer:**
* **Locally:** Backups are saved in a hidden folder on your hard drive. Go to **Backups** in the sidebar and click **Open Backup Folder** to view them.
* **On Cloud:** If Google Drive is connected, backups are uploaded to a folder named **`ScaleERP Backups`** in your Google Drive.

---

### Q4: Can I install ScaleERP on multiple computers?
**Answer:** Each installation requires its own unique license file. Because the license is bound to your specific hardware fingerprint, you must generate a device fingerprint for each computer and request separate license files from support.

---

### Q5: How do I add a new brand or manufacturer to a feed product?
**Answer:**
1. In the left sidebar, click **Products**.
2. Find the product category card where you want to add a brand.
3. Inside that card, click the small dashed box labeled **+ Add Manufacturer**.
4. A window will pop up. Type the manufacturer's name (e.g., *Suguna*) and click **Save**.
5. The new brand will show up inside the product card with a starting stock count of `0`.


---

### Q6: How do I log a payment a customer makes to clear their ledger balance?
**Answer:**
1. Go to **Customer Ledgers** in the sidebar.
2. Select the customer from the dropdown.
3. Click the blue **Add Received Amount** button.
4. Enter the amount paid, select Cash or UPI, add notes, and click **Save**.
5. Their outstanding balance will update instantly.
