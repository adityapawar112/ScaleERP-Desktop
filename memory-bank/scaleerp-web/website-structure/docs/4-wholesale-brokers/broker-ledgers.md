# Checking Broker Ledgers & Payments

The Broker Ledger is your shop's wholesale accounts book. It shows a complete history of how much stock you purchased on credit from a broker, the commissions (brokerage) they earned, and all the payments you have made to settle the accounts.

To open this page, click **Broker Ledgers** in the left sidebar.

---

## 1. Selecting a Broker & Checking Dues
When you want to review what you owe a supplier:

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[The Broker Ledgers screen showing the broker selection dropdown, the dues balance badge, the total brokerage given badge, and the two table sections]*

1. Click the large **Select Broker** dropdown at the top of the page.
2. Select the broker's name from the list.
3. Once selected, two status badges will display:
   - **Total Pending (Red/Green Badge):** 
     - **Red Badge (e.g., `₹45,000.00`):** The money you currently owe this broker for stock purchases.
     - **Green Badge (e.g., `₹0.00`):** You have fully cleared your account and owe nothing.
   - **Total Brokerage Given (Blue Badge):** The total commission/brokerage fees earned by this broker from your purchases.

---

## 2. Recording a Payment to the Broker
When you send money to clear your credit balance with the broker:

1. Click the blue **Add Paid Amount** button under the badges.
2. A window will pop up with the following details:
   - **Payment Method:** Choose **UPI** or **Cash**.
   - **Amount:** Type the exact money paid (e.g., `10000`).
   - **Date and Time:** Leave as current time, or adjust if logging a past payment.
   - **Notes:** (Optional) Add a reference note (e.g., *“Bank transfer ref #1234”*).
3. Click the blue **Add** button.
   - The **Total Pending** dues will reduce instantly, and the payment is logged in the tables.

---

## 3. Understanding the Ledger Tables
The ledger splits history into two clear tables so you can review details with brokers:

### Table A: Amounts to be Paid (Purchases)
* This lists every wholesale purchase transaction logged for this broker.
* It shows the date, invoice notes, the purchase amount in red, and the brokerage commission in blue.

### Table B: Paid Amounts (Receipts)
* This lists all cash or UPI payments you have made to this broker.
* It shows the date, payment method, and the amount collected in green.

*Both tables can be searched using the search box or sorted by clicking the column headers (e.g., Date, Amount, Brokerage).*

---

## 4. Exporting Ledgers & Deleting Entries
* **Exporting to Excel:** Click the green **Download Excel** button. ScaleERP will download a complete spreadsheet of this broker's transaction, brokerage, and payment history.
* **Deleting Ledger Records:** If you log a payment in the wrong ledger or make a typing mistake:
  1. Click **Delete** next to the specific payment or invoice in the ledger table.
  2. Confirm in the safety popup modal.
  3. ScaleERP will remove the entry and automatically recalculate your total outstanding balance.
