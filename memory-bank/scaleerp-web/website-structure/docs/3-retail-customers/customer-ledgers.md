# Checking Customer Ledgers & Payments

The Customer Ledger is your shop's "Khata" book. It shows a complete history of how much credit a customer has taken, what payments they have made, and their current outstanding balance.

To open this page, click **Customer Ledgers** in the left sidebar.

---

## 1. Selecting a Customer & Checking Dues
When a customer wants to settle their account:

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[The Customer Ledgers screen showing the customer dropdown, the red Total Pending dues badge, and the two table sections]*

1. Click the large **Select Customer** dropdown at the top of the page.
2. Select the customer's name from the list.
3. Once selected, a large banner will display their **Total Pending** balance:
   - **Red Badge (e.g., `₹12,500.00`):** The customer owes this amount to your shop.
   - **Green Badge (e.g., `₹0.00`):** The customer has fully cleared their account and owes nothing.

---

## 2. Recording a Payment
When a customer hands you money to clear their credit:

1. Click the blue **Add Paid Amount** button under the dues banner.
2. A window will pop up with the following details:
   - **Payment Method:** Choose **UPI** or **Cash**.
   - **Amount:** Type the exact money collected (e.g., `5000`).
   - **Date and Time:** Leave as current time, or adjust if logging a past payment.
   - **Notes:** (Optional) Add a reference note (e.g., *“Paid by Ramesh's brother”*).
3. Click the blue **Add** button.
   - The customer's **Total Pending** dues will reduce instantly, and the payment is logged in the tables.

---

## 3. Understanding the Ledger Tables
The ledger splits history into two clear tables so you can review details with customers easily:

### Table A: Amounts to be Paid (Receivables)
* This lists every retail bill/invoice created for this customer.
* It shows the date, invoice details (notes), and the bill amount in red.

### Table B: Paid Amounts (Receipts)
* This lists all cash or UPI payments this customer has made to your shop.
* It shows the date, payment method, and the amount collected in green.

*Both tables can be searched using the search box or sorted by clicking the column headers (e.g., Date, Amount).*

---

## 4. Exporting Ledgers & Deleting Entries
* **Exporting to Excel:** Click the green **Download Excel** button. ScaleERP will download a complete spreadsheet of this customer's full transaction and payment history, which you can print out or share with them.
* **Deleting Ledger Records:** If you log a payment in the wrong ledger or make a typing mistake:
  1. Click **Delete** next to the specific payment or invoice in the ledger table.
  2. Confirm in the safety popup modal.
  3. ScaleERP will remove the entry and automatically recalculate their total outstanding balance.
