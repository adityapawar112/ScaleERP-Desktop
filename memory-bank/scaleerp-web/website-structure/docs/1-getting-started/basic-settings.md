# Setting Up Your Shop Details & Bill Templates

Before you start billing customers, you must enter your shop's official details and customize how your invoices look. 

To open this page, click **Settings** from the main sidebar.

---

## 1. Entering Business Details
This section contains the official information that will print at the top of every invoice.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Business Information section of the Settings page showing Business Name, Proprietor Name, Address, and phone number controls]*

1. **Business Name:** Click the box and type your shop's official name (e.g., *Om Sai Feeds & Fertilizers*).
2. **Proprietor Name:** Type the owner's name.
3. **Address:** Enter the complete physical address of your shop or godown.
4. **Phone Numbers:**
   - You can list multiple phone numbers.
   - To add a number, click the blue **+ Add Phone Number** button and type the number.
   - To remove a number, click the red trash icon next to it.

---

## 2. Setting Up WhatsApp Billing Message
When you send a bill to a customer via WhatsApp, the app uses a template to write the message automatically.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[WhatsApp Template section showing placeholder buttons (+ Name, + Date) and the text area with the default template]*

1. Locate the **Invoice WhatsApp Message** box.
2. You can type whatever message you want. Use the quick-insert buttons above the box to put in auto-filling details:
   - **+ Name:** Inserts `{partyName}` (the customer's name).
   - **+ Business Name:** Inserts `{businessName}` (your shop's name).
   - **+ Date:** Inserts `{date}` (today's date).
   - **+ Invoice Number:** Inserts `{invoiceNumber}`.
   - **+ Total Amount:** Inserts `{totalAmount}`.
3. **Example Message:** 
   `Hello {partyName}, thank you for purchasing from {businessName}. Your bill amount is {totalAmount}. Bill No: {invoiceNumber}.`

---

## 3. Uploading Your Logo & Payment QR Code
You can add images to make your printed bills look professional and make it easy for customers to pay via UPI.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Brand Assets section with three grey upload boxes for Company Logo, Header Banner, and Payment QR Code]*

* **Company Logo:** Shows at the top-left of standard printed invoices.
* **Header Banner:** If you have a custom banner design for your shop, you can upload it here.
* **Payment QR Code:** Upload a picture of your shop's GPay, PhonePe, or BHIM UPI QR code. The app will print this QR code directly on the bill so customers can scan and pay instantly.

### How to Upload an Image:
1. Click the blue **Upload** button under the appropriate box.
2. Select your image from your computer and click **Open**.
   - *Note: ScaleERP automatically shrinks and optimizes your image so the app stays fast.*
3. To delete an image, click the red trash icon under the preview.

## 4. Customizing Your Invoice Layout & Printing
Choose how your invoice looks, the paper size, and how many copies print.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Invoice Template & Styling controls showing Layout dropdown, Preset Accent Color badges, and Print Copies radio buttons]*

1. **Default Template Layout:** Click the dropdown and select your paper size:
   - **Standard A4 Corporate:** For large invoices on standard computer paper.
   - **Modern Minimalist Clean:** A clean, modern look for A4.
   - **Compact A5 Half-Page:** Ideal for saving paper (half the size of A4).
   - **Thermal POS 80mm Roll:** For small, fast thermal receipt printers (like grocery store receipts).
2. **Invoice Accent Color:** Click the color box to choose a custom color, or click one of the preset color pills (Royal Blue, Emerald, Crimson, etc.) to match your shop's branding.
3. **Print Copies:**
   - **1 Copy:** Prints one invoice for the customer.
   - **2 Copies:** Prints two identical bills on one press (one for the customer, one for your records).
4. **Multi-Copy Layout Mode:** (Available only when *2 Copies* is selected)
   - **Separate Full Pages:** Prints each copy on a new sheet of paper.
   - **Single A4 Sheet (Dual Stacked):** Prints both copies side-by-side on a single A4 page with a scissor cut line in the middle to save paper.
5. **Custom Footer Tagline:** Type a friendly closing note (e.g., *Thank you for your business!* or *No returns after 3 days*). This prints at the very bottom of your invoices.

---

## 5. Saving and Previewing
At the bottom of the Settings page, there is a **Live Invoice Layout Preview** box.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Live Invoice Layout Preview box showing a sample printed invoice reflecting the custom logo, selected accent color, and QR code]*

1. **Check Your Design:** Look at the preview. As you change colors, upload logos, or write a custom tagline, the preview changes instantly so you can see exactly how the bill will print on paper.
2. **Save Changes:** Once you are happy, click the green **Save All Business Settings** button at the bottom-right. A green toast message will appear saying `Settings saved successfully!`.

---

## Important Warnings

### Unsaved Changes Alert
If you try to click away to another page (like the Dashboard or Inventory) without saving your changes:
* A warning window will pop up saying: `You have unsaved changes in your settings. Are you sure you want to leave without saving?`
* Click **Stay** to go back and save your changes.
* Click **Leave** to discard your changes and go to the next page.
