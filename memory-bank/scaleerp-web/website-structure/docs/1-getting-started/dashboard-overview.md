# Dashboard Overview & Shop Health

The Dashboard is your main screen. It gives you a complete, live bird's-eye view of your shop's financials, inventory health, and sales trends.

---

## 1. Understanding Your Daily Financial Indicators (KPIs)
At the very top of the screen, you will see four key metric cards. These numbers recalculate automatically every time you log a transaction.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[The four KPI cards at the top of the dashboard: Inventory Value, Receivables, Payables, and Today's Sales]*

* **Total Inventory Value (₹):** The total value of all stock currently in your godowns based on your purchase prices.
* **Receivables (₹):** The total money that customers currently owe your shop (pending dues).
* **Payables (₹):** The total money that you currently owe to brokers and suppliers.
* **Today's Sales (₹):** The total billing amount completed today. Use this to track your daily cash flow.

---

## 2. Quick Action Shortcuts
Under the KPI cards, you will find four quick-access buttons. Click these to open the most common screens instantly.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Quick Actions panel on the dashboard showing the four action buttons]*

* **New Sale:** Opens the retail billing page to check out a customer quickly.
* **New Purchase:** Opens the broker page to log a new delivery truck arriving from a supplier.
* **Send Reminder:** Opens the WhatsApp panel to send payment reminder messages.
* **View Reports:** Opens the financial reports page to review sales velocities.

---

## 3. Financial Health & Vitality Index
Located on the right side of the dashboard is the **Business Vitality Index**.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Financial Health card showing the percentage gauge and colored vitality progress bar]*

* **What it is:** A percentage score showing your shop's financial safety.
* **How it is calculated:** It compares your **Receivables** (money owed to you) with your **Payables** (money you owe).
* **Color Indicators:**
  * **Green (above 70%):** Safe. Your receivables are high enough to cover what you owe.
  * **Yellow (40% - 70%):** Caution. Keep an eye on outstanding dues.
  * **Red (under 40%):** Risk. You owe more money than you are bringing in; consider sending payment reminders immediately.

---

## 4. Sales Trends & Stock Charts
These charts help you visualize how your business is growing and which manufacturers are sitting in your godown.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Sales Trend line chart (left) and Stock Distribution doughnut chart (right) side-by-side]*

* **Sales Trend (Last 30 Days):** A line chart that shows your daily sales totals over the last month. Upward curves mean business is growing!
* **Stock Distribution (by Manufacturer):** A colorful doughnut chart showing which feed manufacturers (e.g., Godrej, Suguna, Kapila) make up the bulk of your current inventory.

---

## 5. Alerts & Performance Scoreboards
Scroll down to see active warnings and rankings.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Recent Transactions table (left) and the Low Stock Alerts/Rankings sidebar (right)]*

### Low Stock Alerts
* This box shows any feed item that has **fewer than 10 bags** left in stock.
* Look for the red warning icon. Use this list to contact brokers and order more stock before you run out completely.

### Recent Transactions
* A list of the last 5 invoices created in your shop.
* Shows the Date, Invoice Number, Customer Name, and Bill Amount.

### Top Customers & Top Brokers
* **Top Customers:** Ranked by who owes your shop the most money. Use this list to prioritize who to send WhatsApp payment reminders to.
* **Top Brokers:** Ranked by who you owe the most money to. Use this list to plan your next supplier payments.
