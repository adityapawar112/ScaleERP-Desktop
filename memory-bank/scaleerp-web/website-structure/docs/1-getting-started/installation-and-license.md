# Installation, License Activation & Login

This guide will help you install the ScaleERP application, activate your offline business license, and log in to your shop's dashboard for the first time.

---

## 1. Opening ScaleERP for the First Time
ScaleERP is a desktop application that runs directly on your computer without needing an internet connection during daily business hours.

1. **Launch the App:** Double-click the **ScaleERP** icon on your Windows Desktop.
2. **License Check:** The app automatically checks if you have a valid license. 
   - If this is your first time starting the app, you will see the **Activate License** screen.
   - If your license is active, you will skip directly to the **Sign In** screen.

---

## 2. Activating Your License
ScaleERP uses hardware-bound, offline security. This means your license is locked to your specific computer and does not require the internet to verify.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Activate License screen showing the "Paste License" and "Import File" tabs, the Device Fingerprint box, and the active blue action button at the bottom]*

### Step 1: Copy Your Device Fingerprint
Before you can activate, you must send your **Device Fingerprint** to ScaleERP support to generate your secure license key.
1. Look at the grey box labeled **Device Fingerprint** near the bottom of the form.
2. Click the light-grey **Copy** button next to the long code.
3. Send this copied code to your ScaleERP representative via email or WhatsApp. They will generate your license file (a `.json` file) or send you a license code string.

### Step 2: Input Your License Key
Once you receive your license key from ScaleERP, you can activate it in one of two ways:

#### Option A: Paste License (Recommended)
1. Select the **Paste License** tab at the top of the card.
2. Copy the entire license code text sent by support.
3. Click inside the large box that says `"Paste your license JSON here..."` and paste the code (Press `Ctrl + V`).
4. Click the blue **Activate License** button at the bottom.

#### Option B: Import File
1. Select the **Import File** tab at the top of the card.
2. Click the blue **Browse Files** button in the dashed box.
3. A Windows file window will open. Navigate to where you saved the license file (e.g., your *Downloads* folder).
4. Select the file (ending in `.json`, `.lic`, or `.license`) and click **Open**.
5. The application will process the file immediately.

### How to Know it Worked
* **Success:** A green box will appear saying `License activated successfully! Loading application...` and the screen will automatically change to the Sign In page.
* **Error:** A red box will appear explaining what went wrong (e.g., `"License import failed"` or `"Failed to read license file"`). Check that you copied the complete text, or contact support.

## 3. Signing In to Your Account
Once your license is active, you will see the **Sign In** screen. This keeps your business ledger secure from unauthorized visitors.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Sign In screen showing the Logo, License status badge in green, Username/Password fields, Language Switcher, and the Sign In button]*

### Step 1: Check License Status
* Look at the top-right corner of the Sign In card. You should see a green badge saying **Active** under **License status**. If it is yellow (**Restricted**) or red (**Inactive**), follow the troubleshooting steps below.

### Step 2: Choose Your Language
* In the top-right corner of the entire screen, click the **Language Switcher** dropdown.
* You can switch between **English** and **Marathi (मराठी)**. The screen text, fields, and invoices will instantly translate.

### Step 3: Enter Your Credentials
1. Click the **Username** field and type your username (e.g., `admin`).
2. Click the **Password** field and type your password.
3. Click the blue **Sign In** button.
   - If the password is correct, the app will show a loading spinner and take you to the dashboard.

## Common Questions & Troubleshooting

### What if I forget my password?
1. Click the **Forgot Password?** link located just above the Sign In button.
2. A prompt will appear. Contact your shop proprietor or system administrator. Only authorized personnel can reset passwords to protect your financial logs.

### The app says "License inactive" or "Grace period". What do I do?
* This happens if your annual license has expired or if you have modified your computer hardware. 
* Click **Developer Access** or contact ScaleERP support immediately to renew. You will need to copy your **Device Fingerprint** again and apply a new key.

### What is the "Developer Access" link?
* This link is strictly for ScaleERP installation technicians. It requires a secure developer password and should not be used by store operators.
