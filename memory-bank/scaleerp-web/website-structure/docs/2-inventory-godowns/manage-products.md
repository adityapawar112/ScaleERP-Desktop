# Managing Feed Products & Manufacturers

This guide will show you how to add new feed items, add manufacturers (the companies that make the feed), and edit their names when details change.

---

## 1. How the Inventory is Structured
In ScaleERP, stock is organized in two levels to make Godown tracking simple:
1. **The Product:** The general type of feed (e.g., *Poultry Starter Feed*, *Dairy Premium Mash*).
2. **The Manufacturer:** The brand or company that makes the feed (e.g., *Godrej*, *Suguna*, *Kapila*). 
   - A single product can have stock from multiple manufacturers (e.g., you can have 50 bags of *Suguna Poultry Starter* and 30 bags of *Godrej Poultry Starter* under the same product category).

---

## 2. Adding a New Product Category
When you start selling a new type of feed, you must first create its product category.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Dashed "+ Add Product" card at the top of the Product List, and the Add Product modal pop-up]*

1. Go to the **Products** page from the left sidebar.
2. Under the screen header, click the large dashed box labeled **+ Add Product**.
3. A window will pop up asking for the **Product Name**. Type the name (e.g., *Broiler Finisher Bags*).
4. Click the blue **Save** button.
   - *Note: ScaleERP automatically creates a default manufacturer called "General Manufacturer" under this product so you can start tracking stock immediately.*

---

## 3. Adding a Manufacturer to a Product
If you sell the same feed category from different companies, you should list each manufacturer.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Product card showing "+ Add Manufacturer" dashed box inside it next to existing manufacturers]*

1. Find the product category card on the screen.
2. Inside that card, click the small dashed card labeled **+ Add Manufacturer**.
3. A window will pop up. Type the manufacturer's name (e.g., *Suguna*).
4. Click the blue **Save** button. 
5. The new manufacturer will appear as a small card inside the product card showing a stock count of `0`.

---

## 4. Renaming Products and Manufacturers (Inline Editing)
If you make a spelling mistake, you can correct it instantly without losing your stock logs.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Product card header showing the product name being edited inline with the text box, green checkmark (✓), and red cross (✕) buttons]*

### To Rename a Product:
1. Find the product card and hover your mouse over its header.
2. Click the small **Pencil** icon (`FiEdit2`) next to the product name.
3. The name will turn into a text box. Type the new name.
4. Click the green checkmark (**✓**) to save, or click the red cross (**✕**) to cancel.

### To Rename a Manufacturer:
1. Go to the specific manufacturer's card inside the product card.
2. Click the small blue **Pencil** icon at the bottom of that card.
3. The card title will turn into a text box. Type the new name.
4. Click the green checkmark (**✓**) to save, or the red cross (**✕**) to cancel.

---

## 5. Searching Your Inventory
When your shop gets busy, use the search bar to locate stock counts in seconds.
* Click the search bar at the top of the page that says: `"Search products or manufacturers..."`
* Type what you are looking for (e.g., type *Godrej* or *Starter*).
* The screen will instantly filter to show only products or manufacturers matching your search query.
