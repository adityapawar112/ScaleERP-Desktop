# Responding to Low Stock Alerts

To ensure your shop never runs out of popular feed bags and loses sales, ScaleERP actively monitors your inventory levels and alerts you when stock runs low.

---

## 1. What Triggers a Low Stock Alert?
ScaleERP automatically flags any active feed product that has **fewer than 10 bags** left in stock across all manufacturers.
* *Example:* If you have 5 bags of *Godrej Dairy Feed* and 3 bags of *Suguna Dairy Feed*, your total stock for that category is 8. Since this is less than 10, a warning will trigger.

---

## 2. Where to See Low Stock Alerts
The app highlights low stock in two main locations:

### A. The Dashboard Sidebar (Recommended)
When you log into ScaleERP, look at the right side of the main **Dashboard** screen.
* Under the **Low Stock Alerts** card, you will see a list of items running out.
* Each item shows:
  * The Product Name (in bold).
  * The Manufacturer's name.
  * A red warning icon next to a badge showing the exact number of **Units Left**.

> 📸 **Screenshot Placement:** *[Low Stock Alerts widget on the dashboard showing specific products with red warning badges indicating remaining units]*

### B. The Products Page
* Open the **Products** page.
* Look at the **Total Stock** number on the right side of any product card header.
* Low stock items will stand out so you can identify them while scanning your inventory list.

---

## 3. How to Resolve a Low Stock Alert
Once you see an item in the low stock list, you should reorder it from your suppliers:

1. **Check the Alert details:** Note down the product name and the manufacturer that is low.
2. **Order More Stock:** Contact your broker or supplier to order a new batch of feed bags.
3. **Log the Delivery:**
   - When the delivery truck arrives at your godown, click the **New Purchase** button on the dashboard or go to the **Wholesale Purchases** page.
   - Enter the purchase details (number of units and price) and save.
4. **Alert Clears Automatically:** As soon as the transaction is saved, ScaleERP adds the new bags to your inventory. The stock count will rise above 10, and the red alert will automatically disappear from the dashboard list.
