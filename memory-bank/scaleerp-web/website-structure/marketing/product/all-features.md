# All Features: Built for Precision.

*(A comprehensive look at the ScaleERP platform, grouped by core operational categories in a clean Bento Grid layout.)*

## 1. Inventory & Godown Management (Light Mode)

### Multi-Godown Tracking
Track stock accurately across your retail counter, transit vehicles, and multiple warehouse locations simultaneously.

### Live Stock Audits
Reconcile physical stock with digital records instantly. Apply adjustments with a single click.

### Intelligent Low-Stock Alerts
Set custom minimum thresholds for fast-moving items and get visual warnings before a stock-out occurs.

### Batch & Expiry Tracking
*(Upcoming Feature)* Manage specialized feed variants by manufacturing batches and track expiration dates to minimize dead stock.

---

## 2. Billing & Invoicing (Light Mode with Terracotta Accents)

### Lightning-Fast Checkout
A clean, 3-field entry row designed with predictive text to clear retail queues in seconds.

### Multi-Format Printing
Generate crisp A4 invoices for wholesale distributors, or switch to compact 3-inch thermal receipts for the retail counter.

### Native Language Printing
Seamlessly print item names and terms in standard English or regional Marathi to build better local relationships.

### Custom Branding
Add your business name, logo, and custom terms & conditions directly to your invoice templates.

---

## 3. Ledgers & Party Management (Dark Mode)

### Real-Time Dues Tracking
View complete historical ledgers for every party, including total pending amounts and last payment dates.

### One-Click Settlements
Instantly clear partial or full dues when a party pays via cash or bank transfer. The ledger updates permanently.

### Automated WhatsApp Reminders
Select multiple high-balance parties and send polite, formatted payment reminders directly to their WhatsApp.

### Data Export
Export any ledger statement to PDF or Excel to share with your sales agents or accountants.

---

## 4. Architecture & Security (Deep Navy/Charcoal)

### 100% Offline-First Engine
Powered by a high-performance local SQLite WAL database. Zero cloud latency during retail hours. Never stop billing when the Wi-Fi drops.

### Military-Grade RSA Licensing
Hardware-bound licensing ensures your data and software access is tamper-proof and securely tied to your specific machine.

### Encrypted Cloud Backups
With one click at the end of the day, encrypt your local database and sync it securely to the cloud for ultimate peace of mind.

### Simple Data Migration
Easily import your existing parties, inventory, and ledgers from Excel to quickly transition away from Tally or Marg.
